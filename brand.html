Principles
Quiet chrome, loud data. Paper, rules, and labels are warm and recessive. Numbers are big and saturated. Contrast goes where the eye should go.
Oxblood marks authorship. The 2px rule and the eyebrow. That's it. Never touches status or data.
Mono means metadata. JetBrains Mono for source labels, timestamps, record IDs, section numbers.
White cards on warm paper. Depth from surface contrast, not shadows or borders.
Color always paired. Red/amber/green always appears with a label, arrow, or icon. Never color alone.
