Deal Pulse

A real-time Opp Doctor on every opportunity. Always on, always current, right on the record page. No Slack command, no context switch, no stale data.

Replaces the Opp Doctor Zapier bot (which nobody uses) and extends the Onboarding Check button (which only catches field-level issues). Covers the full picture: validation rules, Apex trigger logic, related record state, and cross-system signals.
